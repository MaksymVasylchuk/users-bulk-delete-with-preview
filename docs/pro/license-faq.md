# License, trial and refunds

## Is there a trial?

Yes: 7 days, with a card or PayPal. You are not charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

## What happens when the license expires or the trial ends?

The Pro features turn off, but nothing is deleted: the recycle bin, scheduled cleanups, saved filters and settings are kept and work again as soon as a license is active. The free plugin keeps working either way.

Scheduled cleanups do not run while the license is inactive.

## Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. You can cancel at any time in **Bulk Users Delete → Account → Cancel Subscription** or in the [Customer Portal](https://users.freemius.com/). The license then stays active until the end of the paid period.

## Can I move the license to another site?

Yes. **Account → Deactivate License** on the old site, then activate it on the new one. Deactivating or deleting the plugin frees the license too.

## What is the refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee: if Pro has a problem that makes it unusable and we cannot solve it, you get a full refund within 14 days of the purchase. Write to us first, so we can try to help.

## Who handles payments?

[Freemius](https://freemius.com/), the reseller of the add-on. You get invoices from the Account page or the Customer Portal.

## Where do I get help?

Reply to the purchase email, or use the contact details in the Customer Portal.
